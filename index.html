Setup Autopilot
by @cryon.tech

A personal content tool for @cryon.tech

Setup Autopilot is a private tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @cryon.tech. It helps make one short video a day about PC gaming setups and send it to that account.

What it does

  • Creates short vertical videos from the owner's own photos and clips, or from free stock media.
  • Uploads each video to the owner's TikTok account. By default it lands as a draft, which the owner reviews and posts in the TikTok app.
  • Reads view, like, comment and share counts for the owner's own videos, so future videos follow what performs well.

It is not offered to other users and does not access any other TikTok account.
