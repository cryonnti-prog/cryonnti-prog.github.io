Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Summary

Setup Autopilot is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @cryon.tech. It handles data from that one account only, keeps it on the owner's own computer, and never sells or shares it.

2. Data the app accesses

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app can access, with the owner's permission:

  • Basic profile info: display name and account ID.
  • Account stats: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Video list and stats: the owner's own videos with their titles, view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Upload access: permission to upload videos to the owner's account.

The app does not access other users' data, messages, contacts or location.

3. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos and to analyze which of the owner's videos perform best, so that future videos can improve.

4. Where the data is stored

Login tokens and video statistics are stored locally on the owner's own computer. The data is not sent to any other server, apart from TikTok itself when uploading or reading stats.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.

6. Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, and can delete the locally stored data by removing the app's data folder.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions? Send a message to @cryon.tech on TikTok.
